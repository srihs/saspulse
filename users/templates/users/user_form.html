Help & Tips
Basic Information

Username and email must be unique. First and last names are required for proper identification.

Role Assignment

Roles define what a user can do. Multiple roles can be assigned to grant combined permissions.

Data Scope

Branches: Assign to Store Managers to limit their view to specific locations.

Schools: Assign to Sales Team to limit product visibility by category.

Permissions

Active: Controls login access.

Staff: Admin panel access.

Superuser: Full system access.

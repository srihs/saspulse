How the Pick List Works
Calculation Logic:
  • Target Stock: Yesterday's Sales × 2.5 days
  • Pick Quantity: Target Stock - (Current Stock + Incoming)
  • Days of Stock: Current Stock ÷ Yesterday's Sales
Priority Levels:
  • Critical: Less than 1 day of stock
  • High: 1-2 days of stock
  • Medium: 2-3 days of stock
  • Low: More than 3 days of stock

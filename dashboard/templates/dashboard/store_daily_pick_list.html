How the Forecasting Works
Forecasting Logic:
  • Analysis Period: Past 7 days of sales
  • Avg Daily Sales: Total sold ÷ 7 days
  • Forecast: Avg Daily × Days × (1 + Safety Buffer)
  • Pick Qty: Forecast - Current Stock
Safety Buffers (Auto Mode):
  • Stable (10%): Sold 5-7 days/week
  • Moderate (20%): Sold 3-4 days/week
  • Volatile (30%): Sold 1-2 days/week
Priority Levels:
  • Critical: Less than 2 days until stockout
  • High: 2-4 days until stockout
  • Medium: 4-7 days until stockout
  • Low: More than 7 days until stockout

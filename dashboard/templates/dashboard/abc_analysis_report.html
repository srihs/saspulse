About This Report

ABC Analysis classifies inventory based on the Pareto Principle (80-20 rule), helping you focus management attention where it matters most.

Formula:

Cumulative Revenue % = (Sum of revenue up to this product / Total revenue) × 100

Classification Boundaries:

  • A Items 0-80% cumulative revenue
  • B Items 80-95% cumulative revenue
  • C Items 95-100% cumulative revenue

Typical Distribution:

  • ~20% of products generate 80% of revenue (A Items)
  • ~30% of products generate 15% of revenue (B Items)
  • ~50% of products generate 5% of revenue (C Items)
How to Interpret This Report
A Items High-Value Products
  • Monitor DAILY - critical to business success
  • Never allow stockouts - maintain safety stock
  • Review pricing and promotions carefully
  • Forecast demand with highest accuracy
  • Priority for inventory investment
B Items Medium-Value Products
  • Monitor WEEKLY - important but less critical
  • Maintain adequate stock levels
  • Standard forecasting methods acceptable
  • Balance between service level and inventory cost
C Items Low-Value Products
  • Monitor MONTHLY - review periodically
  • Consider min/max inventory policies
  • Candidates for discontinuation if slow-moving
  • Lower safety stock acceptable
  • Simple ordering rules (e.g., order when below min)

Strategic Actions:

  • A Items: Premium supplier relationships, expedited shipping
  • B Items: Standard processes and lead times
  • C Items: Bulk ordering, longer lead times acceptable
  • Review classifications quarterly - products can move between categories
Pareto Principle in Action
  • Typically 20% of products generate 80% of revenue
  • Focus on A Items: Small number of products, large revenue impact
  • Don't ignore C Items completely: They may be important for customer satisfaction

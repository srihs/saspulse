About This Report

Stock Turn Rate measures how efficiently you're managing inventory by comparing annual sales to average inventory levels.

Formula:

Turn Rate = Total Sales Quantity / Average Stock on Hand

Benchmark Classification:

  • Excellent > 12 - High inventory efficiency
  • Good 6-12 - Good inventory management
  • Average 3-6 - Average performance
  • Poor < 3 - Slow-moving inventory
How to Interpret This Report

Take these actions based on turn rate performance:

  • Excellent (>12)
    Fast-moving inventory. Ensure adequate stock levels to prevent stockouts.
  • Good (6-12)
    Healthy turnover. Monitor stock levels regularly.
  • Average (3-6)
    Moderate movement. Review pricing and marketing strategies.
  • Poor (<3)
    Slow-moving inventory. Consider promotions, bundling, or clearance.

Risk Indicators:

  • Low turn rate + high stock = Risk of dead stock
  • High turn rate + low stock = Risk of stockout

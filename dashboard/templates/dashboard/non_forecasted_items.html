About This Report

This report shows products in shop/store categories that do not have a forecast generated by the AI forecasting engine.

Skip Reasons:

  • No sales history - Zero sales found in the system
  • Insufficient sales - Less than 10 total units sold
  • Insufficient history - Less than 30 distinct sale days
  • Discontinued - No sales in over 365 days
  • Low frequency - Less than 5 sales per year average
  • Pending generation - Meets criteria but forecast not yet generated
How to Interpret
  • Pending generation items should be investigated - they may need a manual forecast run.
  • Discontinued items may need to be deactivated in Cin7 if they are no longer sold.
  • Insufficient sales/history items are typically new products or very slow movers.
  • No sales history items may be newly listed or incorrectly categorized.

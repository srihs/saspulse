About This Report

Top 20 Best Sellers identifies your highest-performing products to help you focus on what drives revenue.

Ranking Criteria (Sort By):

  • Revenue: Total sales value (Quantity × Unit Price)
  • Units Sold: Total quantity sold in the period
  • Turn Rate: Sales quantity / average inventory (efficiency metric)

Visual Indicators:

1 2 3 Top 3 performers highlighted

Top performer gets a crown icon for quick identification.

Key Insights:

  • High revenue products contribute most to sales but may not be most efficient
  • High turn rate products indicate efficient inventory management
  • Compare across metrics to identify products that excel in multiple areas
How to Interpret This Report

Make strategic decisions based on product performance:

  • Top 5 products:
    Ensure always in stock - these drive significant revenue.
  • High revenue + low turn rate:
    Premium products with high margins - focus on maintaining quality.
  • High units + low revenue:
    Volume products with lower margins - optimize pricing.
  • High turn rate:
    Optimize reorder quantities to maintain efficiency.

Use this report to:

  • Prioritize purchasing budget for top performers
  • Plan promotional campaigns around best sellers
  • Forecast demand for next period
  • Optimize product placement in store/website

Tip: Monitor changes month-over-month to identify trends and adjust strategies accordingly.

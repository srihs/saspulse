About This Dashboard

Inventory Health Score is calculated using a weighted average:

  • Stockout Risk (30%): Percentage of SKUs with less than 30 days of coverage
  • Excess Stock (25%): Percentage of SKUs with more than 180 days of coverage
  • Dead Stock Value (25%): Value of stock with 0 sales in the last 180 days
  • Inventory Turn Rate (20%): Cost of Goods Sold / Average Inventory Value

Higher scores indicate better inventory health. Aim for a score above 70.

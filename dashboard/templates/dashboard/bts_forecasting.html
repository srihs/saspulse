The prediction model calculates average year-over-year growth and projects future sales with confidence intervals. Confidence bounds represent ±20% variance from the central forecast.

How We Predict Your 2027 Sales
What You're Looking At

These numbers show our best estimate of what your Back-to-School sales will be in January and February 2027.

How We Calculate This
1. We Look at Your History

We examine your actual sales from the last 5 years (2022-2026) during the Back-to-School period (January-February).

2. We Find the Pattern

We calculate how much your sales have grown (or declined) on average each year. For example, if sales grew by 10% last year and 8% the year before, we use this pattern.

3. We Project Forward

Using this average growth pattern, we estimate what 2027 will look like if the trend continues.

Understanding the Three Numbers
Predicted Sales

Our most likely estimate based on your historical growth pattern.

Lower Bound (80%)

If things don't go as well as expected, sales might be around this number.

Upper Bound (120%)

If things go better than expected, sales could reach this number.

Pro Tip: These are estimates based on past trends. Actual results may vary based on market conditions, competition, and business decisions you make.

Understanding Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)
What is Customer Lifetime Value?

CLV predicts how much revenue each school/customer will generate over the next 12 months based on their purchase history and trends.

How to Read This Table
Predicted 12-Month Revenue

How much we expect this customer to spend with us in the next year. Higher is better!

Historical Total

Total amount this customer has spent with us since 2022. Shows their overall value.

Value Tier
  • VIP - Over $100,000/year predicted
  • High Value - $50,000-$100,000/year
  • Medium Value - $20,000-$50,000/year
  • Low Value - Under $20,000/year
Churn Risk

How likely this customer is to stop buying from us:

  • High Risk - No orders in 365+ days - Need immediate attention!
  • Medium Risk - No orders in 180-365 days - Follow up soon
  • Low Risk - No orders in 90-180 days - Monitor
  • Very Low - Recent orders - Engaged customer
Growth Trend

Whether spending is increasing or decreasing:

  • Positive % - Customer is spending MORE (growing account)
  • Negative % - Customer is spending LESS (declining account)
How to Use This Information
Priority Actions:
  1. VIP Customers with High Churn Risk - Call them immediately! These are your biggest accounts at risk.
  2. High Value + Growing - Keep them happy! Send thank you notes, priority service.
  3. Medium Value + Declining - Find out why. Offer discounts or check if there's an issue.
  4. Low Value but Growing - Future VIPs! Nurture these relationships.
How It's Calculated:

We analyze each customer's purchase history since 2022, calculate their average monthly spending, identify growth trends, and project forward 12 months. Churn risk is based on days since their last order.

About This Report

Dead Stock identifies inventory items that haven't sold in 584 days (1.6 years) and are still taking up warehouse space and capital.

Criteria:

No Sales in Last 584 Days + Inventory > 0

Why 1.6 Years?

  • Industry standard for identifying obsolete inventory
  • Allows seasonal products to complete multiple cycles
  • Balances between identifying slow movers and giving products a fair chance

Financial Impact:

Carrying Cost Formula:

Units Remaining × Cost Per Unit

This capital could be reinvested in faster-moving products. Additional costs include warehousing, insurance, and potential obsolescence.

Note: Items highlighted in red have carrying costs exceeding $5,000 and should be prioritized for action.

How to Interpret This Report

Prioritize clearance actions based on inventory age and value:

  • High carrying cost items (red highlighted):
    Priority for clearance actions - highest financial impact.
  • Recent dead stock (600-700 days):
    May respond well to promotional campaigns.
  • Very old stock (>1000 days):
    Consider liquidation or donation for tax benefits.

Action Recommendations:

  • Discount 20-50% to clear inventory
  • Bundle with popular items to move stock
  • Donate for tax deduction and warehouse space
  • Return to supplier if possible (check agreements)

Calculate opportunity cost: Money tied up could be invested in fast-moving inventory with better returns.

About This Report

Days of Inventory = Current Stock / Average Daily Sales

Status Classification:

  • Critical 0-10 days (Immediate reorder needed)
  • Low 10-30 days (Order soon to avoid stockout)
  • Healthy 30-90 days (Optimal inventory level)
  • Overstocked > 90 days (Consider reducing orders)

Formula: Days of Inventory = (Current Stock on Hand) / (Total Sales / Number of Days in Period)
